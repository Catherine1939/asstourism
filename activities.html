Seasonal timeline

Summer

December – February

  • Pony trekking in green hills
  • Maletsunyane at full flow
  • Fly fishing for trout

Autumn

March – May

  • Hiking with clear dry trails
  • Photography of golden grass
  • Cultural village tours

Winter

June – August

  • Skiing & snowboarding at Afriski
  • Stargazing on crisp nights
  • 4x4 Sani Pass (snow permitting)

Spring

September – November

  • Wildflowers on the plateau
  • Mountain biking season opens
  • Birdwatching as migrants return
