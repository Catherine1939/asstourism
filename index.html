Why visit

Reasons to choose the Mountain Kingdom

Towering peaks

The Maluti and Drakensberg ranges hold Southern Africa's highest summits — pure alpine air and endless views.

Living culture

The Basotho blanket, the mokorotlo hat and pony trekking are alive in every village you pass through.

Dark skies

At 2,000 m with zero light pollution, Lesotho is one of Africa's finest stargazing destinations.
